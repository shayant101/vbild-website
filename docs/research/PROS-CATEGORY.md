# Pros category — research summary (Oct 13, 2026)

## Why a "Pros" category
Square won micro-merchants with a near-free wedge (the ~$1 reader, no monthly fee, no credit check, flat 2.75%), then vertical bundles (Retail 2017 → Restaurants 2018 → Appointments → Beauty 2024), then an upmarket motion. FY2025: 4.5M+ sellers, $250B GPV, $3.94B Square gross profit (Block 10-K). Services is now Square's slowest vertical (+7% vs F&B +20%, Q2 2026): booking at $0–$49/mo is crowded; custom software for pros is open.

## What pros pay today
Jobber $29–499, Housecall Pro $59–329, GlossGenius $24–148, Booksy $30+$20/staff, Follow Up Boss ~$69/user, Sierra/Real Geeks $300–600, Thumbtack/Angi $25–75 per lead sold to 4–8 pros. Automations, two-way SMS, client portals gated at $149–329. Gap: branded client app, custom quote/intake, cross-tool automations (lead → quote → deposit → job → invoice → review), AI receptionist.
